```
📏 Measurement Guide

Simple measurement guidance for women's custom-stitched outfits. Proper measurements help us create a comfortable and beautiful fit.

```
```

How to Take Measurements

Use a soft measuring tape and keep it comfortably around the body. Do not pull the tape too tightly or leave it too loose. If you are unsure about any measurement, simply contact us on WhatsApp.

```
01

👗 Blouse Measurements

Blouse fitting requires accurate measurements because even a small difference can affect the final fit.

Full Bust

Measure around the fullest part of the bust while keeping the tape horizontal.

Under Bust

Measure around the body directly below the bust.

Shoulder

Measure from one shoulder point to the other shoulder point.

Armhole

Measure comfortably around the armhole area according to the required fitting.

Sleeve Length

Measure from the shoulder point to the required sleeve end.

Blouse Length

Measure from the shoulder near the neck down to the desired blouse length.

02

🧵 Salwar Suit Measurements

For a salwar suit, measurements are generally taken for both the upper garment and the lower garment.

Shoulder

Measure across the shoulders from one shoulder point to the other.

Bust

Measure around the fullest part of the bust.

Waist

Measure around the natural waist area without pulling the tape tightly.

Hip

Measure around the fullest part of the hips.

Kurti Length

Measure from the shoulder to the desired length of the kurti.

Salwar / Pant Length

Measure from the waistline to the required bottom length.

03

👘 Traditional Poshak Measurements

Traditional Rajasthani poshak may require measurements for the blouse, ghagra and other parts according to the design.

Blouse Measurements

Bust, under bust, shoulder, armhole, sleeve and blouse length may be required.

Waist

Measure around the waist where the ghagra or skirt will sit.

Ghagra Length

Measure from the waistline to the required bottom length.

Waist Circumference

Take the complete measurement around the waist for proper fitting.

Dupatta Preference

Mention the preferred length, style and finishing requirements.

Fitting Preference

Tell us whether you prefer a comfortable, regular or closer fitting.

04

💍 Bridal Wear Measurements

Bridal outfits usually require more detailed measurements and may need trial fitting before the final finishing.

Detailed Body Measurements

Accurate measurements are taken according to the design and outfit.

Blouse Fitting

Bust, under bust, shoulder, armhole, sleeve and length may be required.

Waist

Measure the waist according to where the bridal outfit will sit.

Length Measurements

Garment length is measured according to the desired design and footwear.

Sleeve Preference

Mention the desired sleeve length and style.

Trial Fitting

A trial fitting can help identify adjustments before final finishing.

📌 Important Measurement Tips

  • Use a soft measuring tape.
  • Stand naturally while taking measurements.
  • Keep the tape comfortably against the body.
  • Do not pull the tape extremely tight.
  • Wear normal clothing while taking measurements.
  • For fitted outfits, tell your tailor your preferred fitting style.
  • For bridal and special outfits, a trial fitting may be helpful.
  • If you are unsure, contact us before taking the measurements yourself.
