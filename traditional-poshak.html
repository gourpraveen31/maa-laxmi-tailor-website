About The Style

Traditional Poshak

Traditional Rajasthani poshak reflects the rich cultural style of Rajasthan through its colours, fabrics, silhouettes, and detailing.

At Maa Laxmi Women's Tailoring Shop, we create traditional poshak according to your measurements and preferences, with attention to comfort and finishing.

Designs, colours, fabrics, and finishing can be customised to suit weddings, festivals, cultural functions, and other special occasions.
