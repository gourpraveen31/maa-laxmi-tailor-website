Our Approach

From Measurement to Finishing

1. Discuss the design: Tell us your preferred blouse design, neckline, sleeves, fabric, and finishing style.

2. Take measurements: Measurements are taken carefully according to the required garment and fitting.

3. Stitching: The blouse is stitched according to the selected design and measurements.

4. Final fitting: The blouse is checked and adjusted if required before final delivery.
