📏 Measurement Guide

Not sure what measurements are needed? Here's a simple guide for different types of women's clothing.

Blouse

Important measurements commonly needed for blouse stitching:

  • Full bust
  • Under bust
  • Shoulder
  • Armhole
  • Sleeve length
  • Blouse length
💡 Tip: Keep the measuring tape comfortably fitted around the body. Do not pull it too tight.

Salwar Suit

Measurements commonly required for a comfortable custom fit:

  • Shoulder
  • Bust
  • Waist
  • Hip
  • Kurti length
  • Salwar/Pant length

Poshak

For traditional Rajasthani outfits, measurements may include:

  • Blouse measurements
  • Waist
  • Skirt/Ghagra length
  • Waist circumference
  • Dupatta preferences
  • Required fitting

Bridal Wear

Bridal outfits may require additional measurements and fitting:

  • Detailed body measurements
  • Blouse fitting
  • Waist measurement
  • Length measurements
  • Sleeve preferences
  • Trial fitting
